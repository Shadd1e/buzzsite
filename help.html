Help and services

Services
answers.

Clear information to help you use Buzz with confidence.

Buzz Pro is for professionals and businesses who want to present services, connect with customers and grow their commercial presence.
Marketplace helps customers discover products and sellers, understand product information and move towards a purchase through supported transaction flows.
SunPay is the payment experience in Buzz, designed to support clear transaction review, verification and confirmation. Specific payment methods and transaction types are subject to availability and terms.

Account And Safety
answers.

Clear information to help you use Buzz with confidence.

The Privacy Notice explains the information Buzz plans to handle, why it is used and what choices may be available. The published notice must match the actual product and implemented controls. Review it before signing up.
Stop the transaction if possible, do not share passwords or one-time codes, preserve records and contact official support. Contact your financial institution if payment credentials may be compromised.
Never share your password, PIN or one-time verification code with anyone. Treat such requests as suspicious.
