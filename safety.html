TRUST / SAFETY / RESPONSIBILITY

Safer participation takes all of us.

A safer social network takes more than a list of rules. People need clear controls, understandable reporting routes and confidence that concerns will be handled carefully.

  • Recognise suspicious requests and misleading offers
  • Protect passwords, verification codes and personal information
  • Review transaction details before confirming
  • Use official support channels to report concerns

Designed to work in context

People should be able to recognise suspicious behaviour, manage unwanted interactions and report content that breaks the rules. We will publish the actual controls and response process as they are implemented and verified.
