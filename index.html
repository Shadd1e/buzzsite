About

Buzz is built by Aether Jade Innovations Limited. We think a messenger should also help you stay informed, stay safe and find people you can trust close to home.

Is Buzz available yet?

Not publicly. Join the waitlist and we’ll tell you when it opens in your area.

Do I need Pro Mode?

No. Chats, communities and safety tools work without it.

Do people see my phone number?

You choose who can. With BuzzDrop you can exchange your Buzz identity with someone nearby without sharing your number.

How do I become a verified seller, landlord or lawyer?

Turn on Pro Mode in the app, accept the terms, choose what to run and complete verification. Housing and Lawyers require verification before you start.

Lost your phone?

Do these now, whatever happens with the form below.

  1. Call your mobile network and have your SIM blocked.
  2. Change the passwords on your email and banking apps.
  3. Never share a code or PIN with anyone, even if they say they’re from Buzz.

Then request a suspension. A team member reviews it and calls the other contact you give before suspending anything.
