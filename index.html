04 / TRUST IS PART OF THE PRODUCT

A better social experience starts with trust.

Buzz is being shaped around meaningful interaction, understandable privacy choices and practical ways to deal with harmful behaviour. Trust is not a badge. It is something the product has to earn.

  • Clear choices about who can see and interact with your content
  • Visible ways to report concerns and get help
  • Useful services that support people without taking over the social experience
