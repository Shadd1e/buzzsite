Common questions

Short answers.

Is Buzz available yet?

Not publicly. Join the waitlist and we will tell you when Buzz opens in your area.

Do I need Pro Mode to use Buzz?

No. Chats, communities and safety tools work without it. Pro Mode is optional for people who sell, list homes or offer legal services.

Do people see my phone number?

You choose who can see it. With BuzzDrop you can exchange your Buzz identity with someone nearby without sharing your number.

Does Pro Mode cost a subscription?

No. You can turn Pro Mode off whenever you like and you do not pay a subscription to keep using it.

Who can use Buzz?

Anyone who wants a private messenger with community features. Buzz is opening in stages, so join the waitlist for access.

How do I become a verified seller, landlord or lawyer?

Turn on Pro Mode in the app, accept the terms, choose what you want to run and complete verification. Housing and Legal require verification before you can start.

How do I report a problem with a rider or seller?

Use Report or contact, choose "Report" and pick the right category. You will get a reference number.

Privacy and terms

What we collect on this website

Only what you type into a form: your name, contact details, city, and the message or answers you give. We do not ask for ID numbers or payment details here.

Waitlist details are used to contact you about access. Rider details are used to contact and verify you for delivery work. Feedback is used to improve Buzz and to respond if you left contact details. Job applications are used to consider you for a role.

Lost phone

Lost your phone? Let us protect your Buzz.

Four short steps so we know it is really you. Our team reviews every request as a priority and calls you before suspending the account.

Do these right now
  • Call your mobile network and have your SIM blocked.
  • Change the password on your email and any banking apps.
  • Never share a code or PIN with anyone, even if they say they are from Buzz.
Why so many steps?Anyone could pretend to be you. Several checks stop a stranger from suspending or taking over your account.
