DRAFT FOR LEGAL AND OPERATIONAL REVIEW · NOVEMBER 2026
LEGAL REVIEW REQUIRED: This is a structured draft, not a verified statement of Buzz's actual data practices. Confirm every section with the privacy/legal team before publication.

1. Who this notice covers

Identify the Buzz legal entity or entities responsible for the services, contact details and services covered. Explain whether separate service notices apply to Buzz App, Buzz Pro, Marketplace, SunPay and Buzz Logistics.

2. Information processed

Depending on the service, information may include identity and contact details, account information, business profile details, transaction records, order and delivery information, support correspondence, device and technical information, and information users choose to provide.

3. Why information is used

Purposes may include account administration, providing requested services, payment processing, delivery facilitation, verification, fraud prevention, support, service improvement, legal compliance and service communications.

4. Payments and financial information

Explain what payment information Buzz receives, what is handled by payment providers, what transaction records are retained and how SunPay escrow records are handled. Do not claim that Buzz stores full card or bank credentials unless confirmed.

5. Sharing and service providers

Identify actual categories of recipients, such as payment processors, logistics partners, verification providers, hosting providers, analytics services, advisers and public authorities where legally required.

6. International transfers

Describe whether personal information is transferred outside Nigeria and the safeguards relied upon.

7. Retention and security

Explain retention periods or criteria. Describe security measures accurately without promising absolute protection.

8. Your rights and choices

Explain rights under applicable data protection law, how to make requests, identity verification and complaint routes.

9. Cookies and similar technologies

List the cookies, analytics and tracking technologies actually used, their purposes and how users can manage optional technologies.

10. Children

State intended age requirements and how information relating to children is handled in accordance with applicable law.

11. Changes and contact

Provide effective date, change notification process and a monitored privacy contact. Have counsel review against the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and applicable NDPC guidance.
