Know what you are approving.

SunPay is built around a simple principle: important transactions should be clear before they are confirmed. Its secure payment experience is designed to support review, verification and informed approval.

  • Clear transaction information
  • Security checks designed to help protect payment activity
  • Confirmation steps that support informed decisions
  • Escrow functionality for eligible transactions, subject to applicable terms

Designed to work in context

A clear payment flow should show the amount, recipient and purpose before approval. Never share passwords, PINs or one-time verification codes, even with someone claiming to represent a service team.

Escrow availability, eligible transaction types, release conditions, refunds, fees and dispute handling are governed by SunPay’s applicable product terms. Review those terms before using escrow.

Security features reduce risk but cannot guarantee that every transaction will be free from fraud or error. Never share passwords, one-time verification codes or sensitive account credentials.
