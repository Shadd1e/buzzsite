Designed to work in context

Buzz Logistics connects delivery needs with logistics capability — supporting movement within cities, across states and, through the right partners, across borders. Buzz services are designed to complement one another where it makes sense, while keeping each experience clear about its role, availability and terms.

Three kinds of reach

Intrastate: local deliveries within supported cities and states. Interstate: movement between Nigerian states, subject to route coverage. International: eligible cross-border shipments handled through qualified logistics partners.

Availability and pricing depend on route, package dimensions and weight, delivery speed, declared contents, documentation and partner capacity. Customs duties, taxes, permits and restrictions vary by destination.
