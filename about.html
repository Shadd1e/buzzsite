Our story

We are building a social network you can trust.

Buzz is a social network where people can connect, share and join communities, with privacy and safety treated as core parts of the product.

Preparing for public launch

Most of us use one app to chat, another to shop, another to pay and another to deliver. They rarely work well together.

Buzz starts from one belief: online connection should feel more human and respect your personal boundaries.

Our vision

A world where people can connect online without giving up their privacy or safety.

Our mission

Build a social experience that puts people first, gives them understandable choices and makes it easier to participate with confidence.

What guides us

  • Useful over complicated. Every feature should have a clear reason to exist.
  • Be upfront. Say plainly what each service does and what users can expect.
  • People first. Technology should support real needs and relationships.
  • Better together. Connected services should create value beyond isolated features.
