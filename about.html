About / Our thinking

A social network should earn your trust.

Buzz is building a social network where people can connect, share and take part in communities — with privacy and safety treated as essential parts of the experience.

BUZZ / ABOUTPREPARING FOR PUBLIC LAUNCH

The world is full of useful services. But too often, the experience between them is fragmented.

Buzz starts with a simple belief: online connection should feel more human, more considered and more respectful of personal boundaries.

Our vision

A world where people can find meaningful connection without feeling that their privacy or safety is the price of participation.

Our mission

Build a social experience that puts people first, gives them understandable choices and makes it easier to participate with confidence.

What guides us

  • Useful over complicated. Every feature should have a clear reason to exist.
  • Trust is earned. Be clear about what a service does and what users can expect.
  • People first. Technology should support real needs and relationships.
  • Better together. Connected services should create value beyond isolated features.

Find your people. Keep your privacy. Be part of Buzz.
