About / Our thinking

We believe everyday life can work better together.

Buzz is building a connected ecosystem of services designed to bring people, commerce, payments and logistics closer together.

BUZZ / ABOUTPREPARING FOR PUBLIC LAUNCH

The world is full of useful services. But too often, the experience between them is fragmented.

Buzz is being built around a different idea: bring complementary services into one connected ecosystem, while giving each one a clear purpose and a thoughtful experience.

Our vision

A world where everyday connections, services and opportunities are easier to access and work together more naturally.

Our mission

Build useful, dependable digital services that help individuals, professionals and businesses move through everyday life with greater ease and confidence.

What guides us

  • Useful over complicated. Every feature should have a clear reason to exist.
  • Trust is earned. Be clear about what a service does and what users can expect.
  • People first. Technology should support real needs and relationships.
  • Better together. Connected services should create value beyond isolated features.
