1. About these terms

Identify the Buzz contracting entity, registered address, contact details, covered services and how users accept these terms. Explain how service-specific terms interact with the general terms.

2. Eligibility and accounts

Set out minimum age, legal capacity, registration accuracy, account security, verification requirements and circumstances in which access may be restricted.

Prohibit unlawful activity, fraud, impersonation, harassment, misleading listings, infringement, system compromise and misuse of payment or delivery services.

4. Marketplace and Buzz Pro

Clarify whether Buzz acts as marketplace, intermediary or direct seller for each transaction. Set out seller responsibilities for listings, product legality, pricing, fulfilment, returns and warranties. Explain professional verification rules.

5. SunPay and escrow

Publish supported payment methods, limits, fees, escrow eligibility, hold and release conditions, evidence requirements, dispute windows, refunds, reversals and chargebacks. Match the actual product and applicable financial regulations.

6. Buzz Logistics

Define Buzz and partner roles, coverage, prohibited goods, packaging and declaration duties, estimates, failed delivery handling, loss or damage claims, insurance and customs responsibilities.

7. Fees and taxes

Explain applicable fees, display, changes, taxes and tax responsibilities.

8. Intellectual property

Set out ownership of Buzz branding, software and content, user licences and rules for content submitted by users.

9. Suspension and termination

Explain suspension, termination, account closure, pending transactions and records.

10. Disclaimers and liability

Draft fair, enforceable provisions reflecting Buzz's actual role in each service. Do not exclude liability that cannot lawfully be excluded.

11. Complaints and disputes

Provide support escalation, applicable law, dispute resolution, jurisdiction and mandatory consumer rights.

12. Changes and contact

Explain how updates are notified, when they take effect and where users can ask questions or submit complaints.
